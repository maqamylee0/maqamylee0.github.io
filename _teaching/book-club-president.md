As President of the Book Club at CMU-Africa, I lead the organization and facilitation of regular reading sessions, bringing together students around shared intellectual interests beyond the classroom. I curate reading selections, coordinate discussion meetings, and foster an inclusive space where members can engage critically with ideas spanning technology, society, and culture.

To streamline operations, I built and deployed an online Notion library to manage book borrowing and returns for the club. The system includes automated features such as email notifications for due dates and return reminders, making it easier for members to borrow books and for the club to track inventory without manual follow-up.

Skills: community building, event coordination, facilitation, leadership, Notion, workflow automation, systems design.
